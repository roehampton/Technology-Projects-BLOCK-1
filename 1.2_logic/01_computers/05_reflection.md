# Part 5 – Final Reflection
Write **200–300 words** describing your learning experience during this lab.
Consider:
* What surprised you about computer hardware?
  
  A very interesting hardware lab that was attended in which we were able to look at how the computer works. What was most impressive to me was how many of these modern components fit into a single credit card-sized board, it's one thing to be looking at a fully operational computing environment and quite another be looking at one board.
* What components were new to you?
  
  The new for me part of the hardware was the General-Purpose Input/Output(GPIO) header and the System-on-Chip(SoC). The idea of using GPIO pins to enable a computer's software to directly interact with the real world, like sensors, opened the door to a wide range of possibilities for creating applications for the real world.

* What challenges did you face?
  
  The lab had its share of difficulties, however. It took some time to get it up and running without having to use a dedicated display screen, fix loose jumper wires, and fix minor boot configuration problems.
  
* What did you learn about embedded systems?
  
    I felt that i gained a lot of experience on embedded systems here. Embedded systems are systems that are dedicated to a particular use in a particular context that do not have very tight physical and energy limits. Overall, this hands-on experience demonstrated the significance of perfect hardware design and software implementation in the age of smart devices and automation.
