# Part 5 – Final Reflection
Write **200–300 words** describing your learning experience during this lab.
Consider:
* What surprised you about computer hardware?
* What components were new to you?
* What challenges did you face?
* What did you learn about embedded systems?


In this lab, I have acquired knowledge in regard to computer hardware and embedded systems. Prior to the lab, I knew only a little bit about the components inside the computer and I found out that there are quite a lot of components inside that enable computer to perform certain operations. Each component serves its own purpose and even a small component plays a big role in the whole system.

Among the components new to me are: CPU, RAM, storage, motherboard, power supply, and cooling system. Now, I know that the CPU executes instructions, RAM provides temporary storage for running applications, while storage serves for storing the files and OS. Also, I learned that the motherboard is responsible for connecting all the components together and enables them to talk to each other.

The most challenging part in this process for me was identifying and explaining the hardware components. Sometimes it was hard for me to recognize the different parts of computer and figure out their roles in its operations. Taking photos of the components, examining them and creating folders helped me to cope with this task better. Also, uploading my project work on GitHub helped me in learning some project management skills.

In addition to computer hardware components, I got some knowledge about the concept of embedded systems through Raspberry Pi. I realized that an embedded computer is made to perform certain specific actions while using minimum space and energy resources. There are such components and connections as SoC, GPIO, USB, HDMI and network interfaces on a single board of Raspberry Pi.

To sum up, I can say that I learned quite much in this lab about computer hardware and embedded systems. Besides that, this lab gave me practical experience, increased confidence in myself and understanding of computer technology in real life.
