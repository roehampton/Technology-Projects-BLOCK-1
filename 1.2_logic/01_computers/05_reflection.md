* # Part 5 – Final Reflection

During this lab, I learned a lot about computer hardware, software and embedded systems. One thing that surprised me was how many different components are inside a computer and how they all work together. Before this lab, I knew about parts such as the keyboard, mouse and monitor, but I did not know as much about the internal components.

I learned about the CPU, RAM, storage, motherboard, power supply and cooling system. I found out that each component has a different job. For example, the CPU processes instructions, RAM temporarily stores data, and the storage drive saves files and programs. I also learned that the motherboard connects the components and allows them to communicate with each other.

One challenge I faced was understanding the function of each component and identifying its specifications. Some components looked similar, so I needed to look carefully at their labels and understand what they did. However, this helped me improve my knowledge and understand how a computer works.

I also learned about the Raspberry Pi and embedded systems. I found it interesting that many components can be combined onto one small board. This makes the Raspberry Pi compact, affordable and useful for projects such as robotics, smart home devices and sensors.

Overall, this lab helped me understand the difference between hardware and software and how they work together. I also gained a better understanding of the internal parts of a computer and the purpose of embedded systems. I feel more confident identifying computer components and explaining their functions. I believe this knowledge will be useful in my future studies and when working with computers.
