# Part 5 – Final Reflection
Write **200–300 words** describing your learning experience during this lab.
Consider:
* What surprised you about computer hardware?
* What components were new to you?
* What challenges did you face?
* What did you learn about embedded systems?

During this lab, I learned a great deal about computer hardware and embedded systems. One of the most surprising things I discovered was how many different components are required for a computer to function properly. Before completing the lab, I knew about major components such as the CPU and RAM, but I did not fully understand their specific roles or how they work together. I was particularly surprised by the importance of components such as the motherboard, power supply, and cooling system, which are essential for maintaining stable and reliable operation.

Several components were new to me, especially the features of the Raspberry Pi. I learned about the SoC (System on Chip), GPIO pins, microSD storage, and wireless communication modules. The GPIO interface was particularly interesting because it allows the Raspberry Pi to connect to sensors, motors, LEDs, and other electronic devices, making it useful for many practical projects.

One challenge I faced was understanding different number systems and converting between decimal, binary, octal, and hexadecimal. At first, remembering the conversion methods was difficult, but after completing several examples, I became more confident and understood the patterns involved. I also found binary arithmetic and two's complement calculations challenging, but practice helped me improve.

The lab taught me that embedded systems are designed to perform specific tasks efficiently. Devices such as the Raspberry Pi integrate many components onto a single board, making them compact, affordable, and energy efficient. Overall, this lab improved my understanding of computer hardware, logic operations, number systems, and embedded computing technologies, giving me a stronger foundation for future studies in computer science and technology.
