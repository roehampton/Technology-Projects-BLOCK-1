# Part 5 – Final Reflection
Write **200–300 words** describing your learning experience during this lab.
Consider:
* What surprised you about computer hardware?
* What components were new to you?
* What challenges did you face?
* What did you learn about embedded systems?

* This lab was fantastic because I learned a lot about computer hardware, software and embedded systems. Before this lab, I knew some basic things about computers, but I did not understand exactly how the different components worked together.

What surprised me the most was discovering how many different components are inside a computer and how each one has an important function. I could see the internal parts of a computer and understand how they work together to make the system function. For example, I learned about the CPU, RAM, motherboard, storage devices and power supply. I also learned more about the Raspberry Pi and its components, such as GPIO pins, USB ports, HDMI, microSD and network connections.

Some components were new to me, especially the GPIO pins and how they can be used to connect sensors and other electronic devices. I found it interesting that the Raspberry Pi is a small computer that can perform many different tasks.

One of the challenges I faced was understanding the function of each component and remembering their names. At first, some technical terms were confusing, but looking at the pictures and diagrams helped me understand them better. Learning how hardware and software work together was also interesting.

Finally, I learned that embedded systems are computers designed to perform specific tasks inside other devices. They are used in many everyday things, such as cars, washing machines, smart home systems and robots. I also understood why computers like the Raspberry Pi have many components integrated into a single board: to save space, reduce costs and make them more efficient.

Overall, this lab was a great learning experience. It helped me understand computer hardware and software better and gave me more confidence in learning about technology and embedded systems.
