# Part 5 – Final Reflection
Write **200–300 words** describing your learning experience during this lab.
Consider:
* What surprised you about computer hardware?
* What components were new to you?
* What challenges did you face?
* What did you learn about embedded systems?

* During this lab, I learned a lot about the parts inside a computer and how they work together. Before this activity, I knew some basic computer components, but I was not familiar with all of them or their functions. I was surprised to see how many different parts are needed to make a computer work properly.
 
I learned about components such as the motherboard, processor, RAM and storage device. I understood that the processor carries out instructions, RAM holds data temporarily while the computer is running, and the storage device keeps files and programs. I also learned that the motherboard connects the different components and helps them communicate with each other.
One difficulty I faced was understanding the purpose of each component because some of them looked similar to me. I had to take my time and learn the names and functions of each part. After observing them more carefully, I started to understand their roles.

I also learned about embedded systems like the Raspberry Pi. I found it interesting that many components can be placed on one small board. This makes the device compact, affordable and useful for different purposes, such as robotics and automation.

Overall, this lab gave me a better understanding of computer hardware. It also helped me learn how embedded systems work and why they are useful in everyday life. I found the activity helpful because I could connect what I learned in theory with real computer components.
