# Part 5 – Final Reflection
Write **200–300 words** describing your learning experience during this lab.
Consider:
During this lab, I learned a lot about computer hardware, software, and embedded systems. The practical activities helped me understand how the different components of a computer work together. Before this lab, I knew the basic parts of a computer, such as the monitor, keyboard, and mouse, but I did not know much about the internal components.

One thing that surprised me was how many components are inside a computer case and how they work together to perform different tasks. I learned that the CPU processes instructions, RAM temporarily stores data, and storage devices keep files and programs for long-term use. I also learned about the motherboard, power supply unit, and cooling system. Understanding the function of each component helped me see why every part is important for the computer to operate correctly.

One challenge I faced was identifying the internal components and understanding their specifications. Some components looked similar at first, so I had to observe their shapes, positions, and labels carefully. I also learned that it is important to handle computer components safely by disconnecting the power supply before opening the case and taking precautions against static electricity.

The Raspberry Pi activity introduced me to embedded systems. I learned that a Raspberry Pi integrates many important components onto a single board, making it compact, affordable, and energy-efficient. I also learned about GPIO pins, which allow the Raspberry Pi to communicate with electronic components such as sensors and LEDs. This helped me understand how embedded computers are used in robotics, automation, and smart home devices.

Overall, this lab improved my practical knowledge and confidence in identifying computer hardware. It also helped me understand the relationship between hardware and software and gave me a better foundation for future computing and electronics projects.
