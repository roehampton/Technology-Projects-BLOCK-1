| Component | Function |
|-----------|----------|
| SoC |the main processor that controls the Raspberry Pi and performs calculations. |
| GPIO | pins used to connect and control external electronic components and sensors.|
| USB |used to connect peripherals such as a keyboard, mouse and USB storage devices. |
| HDMI | Connects the Raspberry Pi to a monitor or TV to display video and audio.|
| Power | Supplies electrical power to the Raspberry Pi through its power connector.|
| microSD | Stores the operating system, programs and files; acts as the main storage device.|
| Network | Ethernet port used to connect the Raspberry Pi to a wired network or the internet.|
| Wireless | Provides Wi-Fi and Bluetooth connectivity without needing cables.|

## Reflection
Why are many components integrated into a single board in embedded computers such as the Raspberry Pi?
Write **100 words**.
Many components are integrated into a single board in embedded computers such as the Raspberry Pi to make them smaller, cheaper and more efficient. Having the processor, memory, USB ports, network connections and other components together saves space and reduces the need for extra cables. It also makes the device easier to build, transport and use. Integrating components can reduce power consumption and improve communication between them. This makes the Raspberry Pi ideal for projects where space, cost and energy are important, such as robotics, smart home systems and automation. It provides many functions in one compact and affordable device.
