## Reflection
Why are many components integrated into a single board in embedded computers such as the Raspberry Pi?
Embedded computers such as the Raspberry Pi integrate many components onto a single board to make them small, lightweight, and energy-efficient. This design reduces the number of separate parts and connections required, making the device easier to build, maintain, and use. Integrating components can also reduce manufacturing costs and power consumption. A compact design allows embedded computers to fit into devices such as robots, smart home systems, and industrial equipment. Furthermore, having the processor, memory interfaces, and input/output connections on one board improves convenience and simplifies development. Overall, integration makes the Raspberry Pi an affordable and practical solution for many computing and electronic projects.
