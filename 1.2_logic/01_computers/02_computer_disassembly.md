# Part 2 – Computer Disassembly
## Before Opening the Computer
Describe the computer before opening it.
Before Opening the Computer 
The computer used was a desktop model, which had an enclosure covering its internal parts. Prior to opening it, I performed an external inspection on the outside casing, looking at its front part, back part, and sides. I was able to locate the power button, the USB ports, audio input/output ports, and other connection ports. I also inspected the power connections. The casing seemed to be tightly sealed, and I could not see the internal parts of the computer.

## Internal Layout
Open the computer case and observe the internal components.
Describe what you see.

Components after the Computer Case is Opened 
After opening the computer case, some of the vital internal components that I saw were the motherboard, CPU, RAM, storage drive, power supply, and fans. The motherboard is what makes all the other parts interact. The CPU carries out the instructions while RAM is responsible for storing data that is required by the applications. The storage device keeps the files and software installed. The power supply ensures the flow of electricity to the various components.
