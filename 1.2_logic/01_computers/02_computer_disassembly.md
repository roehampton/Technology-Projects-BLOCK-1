# Part 2 – Computer Disassembly
## Before Opening the Computer
Describe the computer before opening it.

Before you open the computer look at the outside of the computer case. I could see the power button, USB port and other connections. The case of the computer protects the innards from dust and damage. I also looked at the different sides of the computer to see how the computer is assembled.

## Internal Layout
Open the computer case and observe the internal components.
Describe what you see.

When I opened the computer case and looked at all the components inside, I could see how the parts were put together. I identified the motherboard, which connects the main parts of the computer. I looked for the RAM, storage drive, cooling fan and power supply. Each part has its own function, and I learn about how the pieces work together.
