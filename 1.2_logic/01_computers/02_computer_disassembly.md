# Part 2 – Computer Disassembly
## Before Opening the Computer
Describe the computer before opening it.


It has a fine mesh section foe ventilation, a power button near the top, USB ports and 3.5mmaudio jacks foe rapid accessory connection.


Side Panel: Solid black metal panel that encloses the internal parts. It is fastened with two large thumbscrews on the rear edge for easy, tool-less removal.

Rear Connections: The main display cables, power and peripherals are plugged in to the motherboard and power supply I/O ports; these are visible cabling found on the back panel.

## Internal Layout
Open the computer case and observe the internal components.

Describe what you see.


After removing the side panel, the core internal parts can be seen, mounted directly within the main frame:


Motherboard: Main circuit board that runs across the rear wall of the case and that connects all the hardware components.


CPU and Cooler: It is near the center-top of the motherboard. A special heatsink fan assembly is placed directly above the main processor.


RAM, or memory: These are the vertical strips of memory that go inside the DIMM slots near the CPU socket.


Power Supply Unit(PSU): Mounted in a dedicated bay at the top or bottom corner, and providing a power cables(24-pin ATX, CPU power, PCIe power)to various parts.


Expansion Slots(PCIe): Slots on the bottom half of the motherboard that allow dedicated graphics/network/sound expansion cards.


Storage and Drive Bays: Mount solid-state drives(SATA/M.2 SSD), or regular HHDs in cages at the front and/or bottom of the machine.
