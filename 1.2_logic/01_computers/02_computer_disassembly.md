# Part 2 – Computer Disassembly
## Before Opening the Computer
Describe the computer before opening it.

Before opening the computer, I observed that it was a standard desktop computer enclosed in a protective case. The outer case appeared sturdy and was designed to protect the internal components from dust, damage, and accidental contact. On the front of the computer, there were power and reset buttons, along with USB ports that allow external devices such as keyboards, mice, and storage devices to be connected. Ventilation openings were also visible to allow airflow and help prevent overheating. From the outside, the internal hardware components could not be seen, but the case housed important parts such as the motherboard, CPU, RAM, storage devices, and power supply. The computer appeared to be in good condition and ready for inspection.

Open the computer case and observe the internal components.

After opening the computer case, I could see the motherboard, CPU, RAM, storage drive, and power supply. The CPU was covered by a cooling fan and heatsink to prevent overheating. The motherboard connected all the components together, while cables supplied power and enabled communication between devices.
