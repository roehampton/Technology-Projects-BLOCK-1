## Internal Layout
Open the computer case and observe the internal components.
Describe what you see.
= After opening the computer case, I could see the different components inside. These include the motherboard, processor (CPU), RAM, storage drive, power supply, and cooling fan.

The motherboard connects all the components and allows them to communicate. The CPU handles instructions, while the RAM temporarily stores data being used. The storage drive keeps files and programs. The power supply provides electricity, and the cooling fan helps prevent the computer from overheating.

I also noticed that cables connect the different components inside the computer.
