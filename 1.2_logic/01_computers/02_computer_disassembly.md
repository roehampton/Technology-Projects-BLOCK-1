## Before Opening the Computer

Before opening the computer, I looked at the outside of the computer case. I could see the power button, USB ports and other connections. The case protects the internal components and keeps them together. I also noticed that the computer had ventilation holes to help keep it cool.

**Image:** `images/system/computer-before.jpg`

## Internal Layout

After opening the computer case, I could see the different components inside. These included the motherboard, CPU, RAM, storage drive, cooling fan and power supply. The motherboard connects the components together so they can communicate with each other. The RAM stores temporary data while the computer is running, and the cooling fan helps prevent the computer from overheating. I learned that each component has an important job in making the computer work.
