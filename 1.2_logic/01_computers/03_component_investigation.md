# Part 3 – Component Investigation

| **Component** | **Function** | **Key Specification** |
|---|---|---|
| **CPU** | The CPU is the brain of the computer. It processes instructions and carries out calculations. | Processor model, clock speed (GHz) and number of cores. |
| **RAM** | RAM temporarily stores data and programs that the computer is currently using. | Capacity (GB), such as 8 GB or 16 GB, and RAM type, such as DDR4 or DDR5. |
| **Storage** | Storage saves files, programs and the operating system, even when the computer is switched off. | Storage capacity (GB or TB) and type, such as HDD or SSD. |
| **Motherboard** | The motherboard connects all the computer components and allows them to communicate with each other. | Manufacturer, model and supported RAM type. |
| **Power Supply** | The power supply provides electricity to the computer's internal components. | Power rating in watts (W), such as 500 W or 650 W. |
| **Cooling** | The cooling system removes heat from the computer to stop the components from overheating. | Type of cooling, such as air cooling or liquid cooling, and fan size if known. |
