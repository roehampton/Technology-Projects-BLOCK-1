| Component | Function | Key Specification |
|----------|----------|------------------|
| CPU | process the information.| clock speed (GHz)|
| RAM |temporary stores data and programs  |memory capacity , GB|
| Storage |stores files, documents, aplications and the operating systems permanently  | storage capacityGB or TB, and SSD or HDD|
| Motherboard | connects all computer components and allows them to communicate with each other| form factor, ATX or micro ATX|
| Power Supply | converts electricity from the wall into power to the computer| power output measured in watts (w)|
| Cooling |keeps the computer cooler and prevents overheating |cooling type air or liquid, and fan size (mm) |
