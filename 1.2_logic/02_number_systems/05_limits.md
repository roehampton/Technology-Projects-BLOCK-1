
## Limits of Binary Representation
**Determine the limits of binary representation:**
   - Largest positive number with 8 bits in unsigned binary = 255 decimal or 11111111 binary
   - Largest positive number with 8 bits in signed binary (two's complement) = 127 decimal or 011111111 binary
   - Smallest negative number with 8 bits in signed binary (two's complement) = -128 decimal or 100000000 binary
