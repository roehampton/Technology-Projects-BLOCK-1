1. **Convert the octal number 745 to:**
   - Decimal
  Convert Octal 745 to Decimal
Step 1: Write the octal number using powers of 8, starting from \(8^0\) on the right.
\[
(745)_8
\]
\[
=7\times8^2+4\times8^1+5\times8^0
\]
Step 2: Calculate each value.
\[
=(7\times64)+(4\times8)+(5\times1)
\]
\[
=448+32+5
\]
\[
=485
\]
Final Answer
\[
\boxed{(745)_8=(485)_{10}}
\]
   - Binary
   Convert Octal 745 to Binary
Step 1: Convert each octal digit into its 3-bit binary equivalent.
Octal Digit	Binary Equivalent
7	111
4	100
5	101


Step 2: Combine the binary digits.
\[
7=111,\quad 4=100,\quad 5=101
\]
Combining them gives:
\[
111 \quad 100 \quad 101
\]
\[
=111100101
\]
Final Answer
\[
\boxed{(745)_8=(111100101)_2}
\]

   - Hexadecimal

Octal	Binary

4	100
5	101

\[

\[
111100101
\]
\[
=0001 \quad 1110 \quad 0101
\]
Step 3: Convert each 4-bit group into hexadecimal.
Binary	Hexadecimal

Final Answer

2. **Convert the hexadecimal number 3F9 to:**
   - Decimal
Convert Hexadecimal 3F9 to Decimal
Step 1: Write the hexadecimal number using powers of 16, starting from \(16^0\) on the right.
\[
(3F9)_{16}
\]
In hexadecimal:
- \(3 = 3\)
- \(F = 15\)
- \(9 = 9\)
Therefore,
\[
=3\times16^2+15\times16^1+9\times16^0
\]
Step 2: Calculate each value.
\[
=(3\times256)+(15\times16)+(9\times1)
\]
\[
=768+240+9
\]
\[
=1017
\]
Final Answer
\[
\boxed{(3F9)_{16}=(1017)_{10}}
\]



   - Binary
 
Convert Hexadecimal 3F9 to Binary
Step 1: Convert each hexadecimal digit into its 4-bit binary equivalent.
Hexadecimal Digit	Binary Equivalent
3	0011
F	1111
9	1001


Step 2: Combine the binary digits.
\[
3=0011,\quad F=1111,\quad 9=1001
\]
Combining them gives:
\[
0011 \quad 1111 \quad 1001
\]
\[
=001111111001
\]
Step 3: Remove the leading zeros.
\[
=1111111001
\]
Final Answer
\[
\boxed{(3F9)_{16}=(1111111001)_2}
\]
   - Octal
Convert Hexadecimal 3F9 to Octal
Step 1: Convert the hexadecimal number to binary.

Each hexadecimal digit is converted into 4-bit binary.
Hexadecimal	Binary
3	0011
F	1111
9	1001


Therefore,
\[
(3F9)_{16}=(001111111001)_2
\]
Step 2: Group the binary digits into groups of three, starting from the right.
\[
001 \quad 111 \quad 111 \quad 001
\]
Step 3: Convert each 3-bit binary group into its octal equivalent.
Binary	Octal
001	1
111	7
111	7
001	1


Step 4: Combine the octal digits.
\[
=1771
\]
Final Answer
\[
\boxed{(3F9)_{16}=(1771)_8}
