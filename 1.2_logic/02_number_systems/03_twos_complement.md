## Two's Complement
**Find the two's complement of the binary number:**
   - 101010
Step 1: Invert all the bits
Change every 1 to 0 and every 0 to 1.
Original:   101010
Inverted:   010101

Step 2: Add 1
     010101
   +      1
   --------
     010110
\[
